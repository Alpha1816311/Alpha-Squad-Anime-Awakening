PROJECT ALPHA SQUAD: ANIME AWAKENING
Build a premium anime recruitment experience with cinematic effects, attractive character design, and a working application system.

You are acting as a principal full-stack engineer, AAA anime game creative director, expert motion designer, senior UI/UX architect, and backend engineer.

Your mission is to build a complete, visually spectacular, mobile-first application named:

ALPHA SQUAD — ANIME AWAKENING

This is a recruitment platform for building a serious, ambitious, multidisciplinary hackathon team. Applicants may come from any college, course, academic year, gender, or skill level.

The experience should feel like entering a high-budget anime action RPG, awakening a unique power, choosing a character class, completing a recruitment quest, and earning the opportunity to join an elite technology squad.

This must be a real, functional application, not merely a landing-page mockup.

IMPORTANT:

Inspect the existing environment and available tools before implementing anything.
